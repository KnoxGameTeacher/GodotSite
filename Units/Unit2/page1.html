Section 1: Project Setup & Character Selection

Create the "Plenty o' Platforms" project, choose a platformer asset pack from Kenney, and import it into Godot.

Section Progress: 0%
1

Creating the Project

  • Open Godot and create a new project
  • Name the project "Plenty o' Platforms"
  • Select "Forward+" as the renderer
  • Click "Create"
💡 Pro Tip: "Plenty o' Platforms" is a classic 2D platformer where you will jump between platforms, collect items, and reach a goal.
Figure 1.1: Creating the Plenty o' Platforms project
2

Selecting Your Visual Theme

Kenney offers many excellent 2D platformer asset packs. All are free and use the CC0 license, meaning you can use them in any project, even commercial games.

Visit the Kenney platformer assets page:

  • https://kenney.nl/assets?search=platformer

Things to consider:

  • Make sure you have a character, some items or other collectibles, and some enemies in your pack.
  • Many of the packs are extensions of Platformer Art Deluxe, and they go together nicely
  • You can mix and match multiple sets because Kenney uses a unified color palette generally.
  • Our project is 2D, be sure the one you choose is 2D and not 3D
💡 Design Choice: Pick a theme that excites you! Your choice will affect the look of your player character, platforms, and environment.
Figure 1.2: Selecting a platformer asset pack from Kenney
3

Getting the Assets

  • Click the "Download" button for your chosen pack
  • The pack will download as a ZIP file
  • Extract the ZIP file to a location you can find easily
  • Open the extracted folder to see the contents
Figure 1.3: Extracted asset pack with organized folders
4

Dragging Assets into Godot

  • In your Godot project, locate the FileSystem dock
  • Right-click and select "Create Folder"
  • Name the folder "assets"
  • Open your file explorer (Windows) or Finder (Mac)
  • Navigate to your extracted Kenney asset pack folder
  • Drag the entire extracted folder into the assets/ folder in Godot
  • Wait for Godot to import all the files
⚠️ Important: Drag the whole extracted folder, not just individual files. This preserves Kenney's organized folder structure.
Figure 1.4: The imported asset pack in Godot's FileSystem dock
