🏆 Bonus Challenge 1

When a prey animal is hunted, spawn a "meat" pickup that the player must collect.

  • Meat pickup spawns at the prey's position when hunted (2 points)
  • Player must walk over the pickup to collect it (Area3D detection) (1 point)
  • Collecting meat restores hunger (e.g., +25 hunger) (1 point)
  • Meat pickup has a visual (mesh or sprite) and despawns after 10 seconds (1 point)
⭐

🏆 Bonus Challenge 2

Add health systems to prey and predator animals.

  • Prey animals require 2 hits to be fully hunted (1 point)
  • Predator animals require 3 hits to be defeated (1 point)
  • Visual feedback for each hit (flash red, change color, etc.) (2 points)
  • Defeated predators also drop a consumable or have some consequence (1 point)
