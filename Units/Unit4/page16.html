Section 16: Sound Effects and Music

Add sound effects for every event and looping background music

Where can I get free sounds?

There are loads of free assets available, below is just a sampling of options. The first few are not just the sound files, they are tools to create your own. You'll need one sound per event for Steps 1 and 2, and one music loop for Step 3.

  • ChipTone: generate retro sound effects in your browser, then download them. Sounds you make are free to use under CC0.
  • rFXGen: generate effects from presets like Coin, Shoot, and Explosion, then export as .wav.
  • Kenney audio: ready-made sound effect packs, free to download.
  • Bosca Ceoil Blue: compose your own background music loop with a beginner-friendly step sequencer, then export as .wav.
  • SLAMY: organize, trim, and layer sounds you already have into new ones.
  • Free Tagged Music Assets on Itch: this is just a link to the search, changes over time.
  • Free Tagged Sound Effects Assets on Itch: this is just a link to the search, changes over time.

One Player per Sound

  • First source your sound files. Free packs like Kenney's game sounds are a good source, but you can look anywhere, just pay attention to the use licenses. Pick one sound per event. I will code the most general use cases here.
  • Add an AudioStreamPlayer child to each scene that makes a sound, and drag your sound file into its Stream property:

Placement -> PlaceSound
Tower scene -> FireSound
Enemy scene -> FenceHitSound
WaveManager -> WaveStartSound, WaveCompleteSound
HUD -> ClickSound
  • AudioStreamPlayer plays globally, the same volume everywhere. AudioStreamPlayer3D plays from a position in the world. Since our camera is static, you can use AudioStreamPlayer for most sounds.
  • The enemy death sound gets no player. The enemy is freed when it dies, and a child player would die with it. Get a sound for death, but don't add a player for it.
  • Add a death sound export to scripts/enemy.gd, and drag your death sound into it in the Inspector:

@export var death_stream: AudioStream

Triggering Sounds

  • Add the reference to scripts/placement.gd, and play it at the end of try_place, after the zone is marked occupied:

@onready var place_sound = $PlaceSound

	place_sound.play()
  • Add the reference to scripts/tower.gd, and play it in fire_projectile(), after the projectile is placed:

@onready var fire_sound = $FireSound

	fire_sound.play()
  • Add the reference to scripts/enemy.gd. The fence hit sound plays from a child player. The death sound spawns a temporary player, because the enemy is freed on death:

@onready var fence_hit_sound = $FenceHitSound

# In take_damage, inside the kill check, before get_parent().queue_free():
	var player = AudioStreamPlayer.new()
	player.stream = death_stream
	get_tree().root.add_child(player)
	player.play()
	player.finished.connect(player.queue_free)

# In the camp block, after Global.fence_durability -= damage:
	fence_hit_sound.play()
  • The temporary player frees itself when the sound finishes. Nothing lingers in the tree.
  • Add the references to scripts/wave_manager.gd. Play the start sound at the top of start_wave, and the complete sound beside the Wave complete print:

@onready var wave_start_sound = $WaveStartSound
@onready var wave_complete_sound = $WaveCompleteSound

	wave_start_sound.play()

	wave_complete_sound.play()
  • Play the complete sound beside the print, so it starts before the summary pauses the tree.
  • Add the reference to scripts/hud.gd, and play it as the first line of the Start Wave handler. Add the same line to each tower handler too:

@onready var click_sound = $ClickSound

func _on_start_wave_button_pressed():
	click_sound.play()
	wave_manager.start_wave()

One Track Under Everything

  • Music is one long track, not one sound per event. Pick a track that loops cleanly.
  • Add an AudioStreamPlayer to your main scene and name it "Music" and drag your track into its Stream property.
  • Turn on Autoplay on Music in the Inspector, so it starts when the main scene loads.
  • If you want your music to play when the game is paused, change the Process Mode to Always in the inspector.
  • Loop is an import setting, not a player setting. Select the music file in the FileSystem dock, open Import, turn on Loop, and reimport. If the track stops after one play, Loop is off in Import.
  • Music is on the main scene, not on a wave or a tower, so it plays across waves without restarting.

Testing Sound and Music

  • Start the game. The music plays under everything, and loops instead of stopping. All sounds should play as expected.
  • Kill an enemy. The death sound plays fully, even though the enemy is gone.
  • Complete a wave. The complete sound rings out over the summary panel.
💡 If the sounds will not play:
  • No sound at all: the stream is empty. Check the Inspector stream on that player.
  • Death sound cuts off instantly: the player is a child of the enemy, and the enemy is freed on death. Use the one-shot pattern from Step 2.
  • Sound plays but the wrong one: the stream on that player is the wrong file. Check each player's stream.
  • Music never starts: Autoplay is off. Check Music in the Inspector.
  • Music stops after one play: Loop is off in Import. Reimport the track with Loop on.
