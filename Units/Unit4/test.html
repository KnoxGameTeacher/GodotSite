Section 20: Upgrades and Selling (8 points)
1

Verification Checklist

  • Each tower scene has a ClickArea Area3D with a CollisionShape3D covering the tower
  • tower_clicked signal connects in try_place and in load_towers
  • Clicking a placed tower prints its name and level
  • Clicking the ghost preview prints nothing, the preview guard ignores it
2

Verification Checklist

  • select_material.tres exists with Transparency Alpha, yellow Albedo at 0.5, and Emission on
  • The material loads with const SELECT_MATERIAL = preload("res://materials/select_material.tres")
  • Clicking a tower shows its ring, clicking a second tower moves the ring
  • Clicking the selected tower again hides the ring
3

Verification Checklist

  • Level 1 to 2 costs 75 percent of the tower's base cost
  • Damage rises by 1.5 times and cooldown falls by 1.5 times
  • A +2 badge appears above the tower after the first upgrade
  • A second upgrade costs 150 percent of the base cost and the badge reads +3
4

Verification Checklist

  • max_level is 5
  • Pressing U at level 5 does nothing, and the Output panel reports it cannot upgrade
  • The badge never reads above +5
5

Verification Checklist

  • Refund covers the base cost plus every upgrade bought
  • The tower vanishes and the zone takes a new tower
  • A sold tower does not return after saving and loading
6

Verification Checklist

  • Save with a level 3 tower, then load the save
  • The tower comes back level 3, with its +3 badge showing
  • Selling the loaded tower refunds the full invested amount
Bonus Challenges (Optional - up to 10 points)

Bonus Challenge 1

Make level 5 look special, so a maxed tower stands out from the rest.

  • At level 5 the badge turns gold or reads "MAX" instead of "+5"
  • A message shows when a tower reaches level 5, print or label
💡 Hint: Check the level against max_level in apply_upgrade, where the badge text is already set.

Bonus Challenge 2

Show what the selected tower is, right on the HUD.

  • Selecting a tower shows its name, level, damage, and cooldown on the HUD
  • The card updates when the selection changes, and clears on deselect
💡 Hint: The HUD already holds a reference to Placement, so it can read placement.selected_tower and its stats.

Bonus Challenge 3

Upgrades and selling only happen between waves, never mid-wave.

  • Upgrades only work between waves (1 point)
  • Selling only works between waves (1 point)
  • A message tells the player when an upgrade or sale is refused mid-wave (1 point)
💡 Hint: Placement can read the WaveManager the same way the HUD does, with $"../WaveManager", and wave_active is true while a wave runs. Guard upgrade_selected and sell_selected with it, the same guard pattern as try_place.

Bonus Challenge 4

The GDD lists achievements as a Could Have. Build a tracker that remembers them.

  • Track at least three achievements in Global, ex. first upgrade, a tower reaching level 5, first sale (2 points)
  • Show earned achievements on the game-over screen (2 points)
  • Achievements persist across saves, so a reloaded game keeps them (1 point)
💡 Hint: Store earned achievements in Global, the same autoload that holds gold and the wave count, and save them with the game so a reloaded game keeps them.
